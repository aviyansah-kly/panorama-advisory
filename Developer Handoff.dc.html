Website build specification

This package contains a complete, finished design for the Panorama website in French and English — twenty pages of production-ready markup, copy, imagery and typography. It is a reference build, not a deployable site. This document describes what is included, what has to be rebuilt, and what remains to be connected.

01 — What you have received

Each page is a single self-contained HTML file that opens directly in a browser. All layout is inline CSS, so nothing depends on an external stylesheet or build step. The markup, the type scale, the colour values and the final approved copy are all readable in the source.

The assets/ folder holds the two wordmark files (ink for light backgrounds, paper for dark). The uploads/ folder holds all photography, already compressed for web.

02 — Design system

Two typefaces, both from Google Fonts. Archivo is a variable font and the design uses a width axis setting of 94 on all display type — this matters, and a static Archivo will not reproduce it.

Role Family Notes
Display and bodyArchivo, weights 400 and 500Variable; font-variation-settings: 'wdth' 94
Numerals and metadataIBM Plex Mono, weights 400 and 500Section numbers, areas, prices

Type scale

All display sizes are fluid via clamp(); the values below are the desktop maximums. 11px uppercase with letter-spacing for labels, section numbers, navigation and the footer. 11px IBM Plex Mono for numerals and metadata. 13px for footer links. 16px for contact details. 18px for running body text at line-height 1.66. 22px for service titles, 26px for legal subheadings. 38px for the inline heading in numbered lists. 62px and 94px for section headings. 112px for page headlines. Do not introduce intermediate sizes.

Layout is a twelve-column grid with a 40px page gutter and fluid column gaps. Section headers use a hairline rule with the section number on the left and a short label on the right. Nothing has rounded corners; nothing has a drop shadow. That restraint is the design.

04 — Still to be connected

These items are outside the design and require your work.

Item What is needed
Contact formThe form is styled but inert. Wire the submission, add spam protection and server-side validation, and confirm where submissions are delivered. Fields are name, organization, email, phone, subject and message.
Property listingsFour active listings are hard-coded on the Propriétés and Properties pages, in both languages. Images and detail pages already point to the existing management application. Decide whether this table stays manual or is fed from that system.
LinkedIn linksEvery footer LinkedIn link is a placeholder. The company page URL is required.
Language switchEvery page carries a working FR / EN switch to its counterpart. Add hreflang tags and decide whether to redirect first-time visitors by browser language. French must remain the default.
MetadataPage titles and meta descriptions are written on all content pages, in both languages. The privacy and legal pages carry titles only. Open Graph tags, a share image, a favicon and a sitemap are still to be produced.
AnalyticsNone installed. Note that the privacy policy already commits to a cookie notice, so whatever is installed must be consistent with it.
HostingDomain, TLS certificate, redirects and a staging environment for review before launch.

05 — Open before launch

These items are the client’s responsibility and block go-live rather than development.

Team photography

The seven team portraits on Cabinet and EN-Firm load from agencepanorama.ca. They must be brought into the new asset library before the old site is retired, or the profiles will break.

Testimonial approvals

Four client testimonials appear on the home pages and one on the Indigenous real estate pages. Written approval from each organization should be on file before publication.

“Unique au Canada” / “Unique in Canada”

The claim heads both firm pages. It rests on holding an authorized agency licence, a federal security clearance and a federal contract at the same time. Have counsel confirm the wording is defensible as stated.

Photography licences

Two images carry iStock filenames and two are of unknown origin. All four need a licence confirmed in Panorama’s name for commercial web use.

Legal review

The privacy policy and legal notices are drafted in both languages and have not been reviewed by counsel. Quebec’s Law 25 and OACIQ disclosure requirements both apply.

Credential verification

Licence number H0531, the seven team titles and the English forms used for OACIQ designations should be checked against the register. Note that the French pages use “accréditée” and “habilités” as editorial wording where the OACIQ term of art is “autorisée”.
