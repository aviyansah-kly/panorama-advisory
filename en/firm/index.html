The Firm

Unique in Canada.

The only certified First Nations real estate agency that represents both corporate clients and the Government of Canada, as its agent for the disposal of federal properties in Quebec.

Team

Benoit Loyer

President, Chartered Real Estate Broker

Benoit has more than 25 years in commercial, industrial and institutional real estate, as a broker and investor. Public companies, financial institutions, governments and First Nations leaders consult him before their major real estate commitments. A member of the Mi’gmaq community of Listuguj, he is an instructor at HEC Montréal’s First Nations Executive Education school.

Julia Cloutier, MBA

Vice-President, Advisory and Management

Julia has more than 20 years of experience managing national multi-site portfolios at VIA Rail, Metro and Sobeys, where she led lease administration and building performance. She holds an MBA in real estate from UQAM and leads the Advisory Division and its strategy, feasibility and asset optimization mandates.

Susan Levinger

Vice-President, Commercial Real Estate Broker

Susan has nearly 25 years of combined experience in real estate brokerage and business development. She leads the sale, acquisition and leasing of commercial properties for companies and institutions of all sizes. She structures the terms, leads the negotiations and follows every file through to signing.

Benoit Lacoste

Vice-President, Commercial Real Estate Broker

Benoit has more than 35 years in commercial, industrial and income property transactions. He has acted for owners and occupiers in leasing, sales and acquisitions, and has held income properties on his own account. He works on deal structuring and negotiation within the Brokerage Division.

Kahs Sky‑Deer

Vice-President, Relations and Corporate Services

Former Grand Chief of Kahnawà:ke, Kahs served nearly twenty years on the Mohawk Council. She advanced her nation’s positions with Ottawa and Quebec City on jurisdiction, territory and economic development. At Panorama, she builds connections between Indigenous and non-Indigenous businesses.

Carla Brown

Marketing Director, Real Estate Broker

Active in real estate for nearly 20 years, Carla designs the positioning, documentation and exposure strategy for every property brought to market. Her work brings out each property’s strengths for the targeted buyers. She also represents buyers and sellers in their transactions.

Nadezhda Bundova

Director, Real Estate Operations Compliance

Nadezhda oversees the regulatory compliance of all operations in both divisions. She reviews the records, supporting documents and OACIQ-prescribed forms at every stage, and coordinates the transmission of documents to notaries and other professionals. No mandate moves forward without her approval.

History

Thirty-five years of expertise, a First Nations business succession.

  1. 1990

    Trillion is founded in Laval. Companies and institutions entrust it with the sale, leasing and management of their buildings.

  2. 2010

    Benoit Loyer joins as a chartered real estate broker and property manager. His clients include Ivanhoé Cambridge, Cominar REIT, InnVest Hotels, Metro Inc. and HEC Montréal.

  3. 2025

    He acquires the firm, renames it Panorama and establishes it in Kahnawà:ke. Mandates, clients and team are retained.
