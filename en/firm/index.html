Succession Since 1990

The handover happened from within.

1990

Trillion, today Panorama, is founded in Laval. Companies and institutions retain it for brokerage and property management mandates.

2010

Benoit Loyer joins as agency executive officer and chartered broker. He leads major mandates for Ivanhoé Cambridge, Cominar, Metro inc. and HEC Montréal.

2025

Benoit Loyer acquires the company. The Trillion name is replaced by Panorama. Ongoing mandates continue with the same team.

Profiles

Benoit Loyer

President, Real Estate Strategy, Advisory & Transactions
Agency Executive Officer, Chartered Real Estate Broker

Benoit has more than 25 years in business, management and commercial, industrial and institutional real estate. His record combines major transactions with property and asset management mandates for publicly traded companies, pension funds and institutions. He is a member of the Mi'gmaq community of Listuguj and a guest lecturer at First Nations Executive Education at HEC Montréal. He leads the firm’s contract with the Government of Canada.

Julia Cloutier, MBA

Vice-President, advisory and management

Julia has more than 20 years in property and asset management at VIA Rail, Metro and Sobeys, where she ran multi-site portfolios, lease administration and asset performance. She holds an MBA in real estate from UQAM, leads the Advisory and management division and carries out portfolio, feasibility and planning mandates.

Susan Levinger

Vice-President, commercial real estate broker

Susan leads negotiations, structures the conditions and holds the file through to closing. She works with corporations and institutions on leasing, sale and acquisition.

Benoit Lacoste

Vice-President, commercial real estate broker

Benoit has more than 35 years in commercial, industrial and rental building transactions. He has acted for owners and occupants on leasing, sale and acquisition mandates, and has held rental buildings on his own account. He works on transaction structure and negotiation in the Brokerage division.

Kahsennenhawe Sky-Deer

Vice-President, relations and corporate services

Kahsennenhawe served close to twenty years in elected office at the Mohawk Council of Kahnawà:ke, including as Grand Chief. She led negotiations with the federal and provincial governments on jurisdiction, territory and economic development. She knows from the inside the processes that govern a community real estate decision.

Carla Brown

Marketing Director, real estate broker

Carla has close to 20 years in real estate. A broker, she prepares the positioning, documentation and exposure of buildings before they reach the market. She also carries out buyer, seller and tenant representation mandates.

Nadezhda Bundova

Director, real estate operations compliance

Nadezhda ensures compliance on mandates in both divisions. She verifies documentation, registers and mandatory forms at every stage of a file, and sees to the agency’s regulatory obligations as well as its contractual commitments.
