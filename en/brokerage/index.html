Scope of practice

Panorama brokerage activity is carried out in Quebec, where the agency is authorized by the OACIQ. Elsewhere in Canada, brokerage is handled by a broker licensed in the province concerned, through our membership in the Canadian Real Estate Association, and Panorama stays on the file with the client. Advisory and management services are not subject to that limit and are delivered across the country.
